QNotch Calculator help

Calculating

Type one calculation per line. Each result appears on the right of its line and updates as you type.

12 * 3.5Arithmetic
price = 40A variable used by later lines
price * 3Lines can refer to earlier variables
5 km in milesUnits and conversions
2027-03-01 + 30 daysDates
double(x) = x * 2A function, saved for every sheet

Dice lines roll when you press Enter at the end of the line, and stay rolled until the dice change. Ctrl+Enter rolls the current line again.

10d6 + 4Dice plus a modifier
2d20kh1 + 5Keep the highest (kl lowest; dh/dl drop)
crit 2d6 + 3Critical hit: the dice are rolled twice, the modifier added once
crit! 2d6 + 3Crunchy critical: the dice at maximum plus a roll

Click a result, or focus it and press Enter, to copy it. Press F1 on a focused result to see how it was calculated.

Sheets

Ctrl+N or +New sheet
F2Rename the variable at the cursor everywhere it is used, otherwise the sheet
Ctrl+W, middle-click a tab or its xClose the sheet; Undo brings it back for a few seconds
Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+TabNext or previous sheet
Right-click a tabRename, version history or close
Ctrl+C or Ctrl+X with nothing selectedCopy or cut the whole line; Ctrl+V then inserts it as a new line
F1This help

Sheets save automatically. If a save fails, the Calculator says so and keeps the sheet open.

Settings

QNotch Settings, Calculator chooses the workday calendar, currency rates, a default currency, text size and number format. Light and dark follow QNotch. With a default currency, a line such as 800 usd shows its converted amount; the line still holds USD for later lines, and a line that names its own target, such as 800 usd in eur, keeps it.

Your data

Sheets, functions and settings stay on this computer in the calculator folder of QNotch's data folder. The Calculator needs no account. It uses the network only when you ask it to, or for the daily exchange rate update if you turn that on. Neither sends sheet text or amounts.
